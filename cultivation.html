Field-Level Masterclass

The 6-Stage Cultivation Blueprint

Achieve maximum crop health, lower input costs, and protect produce quality with our end-to-end agronomic roadmap.

01

Know Your Soil

Healthy soil is the foundation of high-yield farming. Before buying seeds or applying complex fertilizers, perform a representative soil test to know your field’s exact nutrient status.

Action Checklist

  • • Collect zig-zag composite soil samples at 15 cm depth across the field.
  • • Check Soil Health Card for pH (ideal 6.5–7.5), Organic Carbon, Available Nitrogen, Phosphorus & Potash.
  • • Apply Gypsum in alkaline/sodic soils or agricultural lime in acidic soils as advised by KVK.
02

Prepare & Plant

Good tilth allows early root penetration and moisture retention. Proper seed treatment shields young germinating seeds from soil-borne fungi and early sucking pests.

Action Checklist

  • • Conduct deep summer ploughing to expose hibernating larvae and solarize weed roots.
  • • Treat certified seeds with Trichoderma viride (10g/kg) or recommended bio-formulations.
  • • Maintain recommended seed-to-seed spacing (avoid overcrowding to curb fungal damp-off).
03

Help It Establish

The first 25 days dictate final plant population. Monitor field emergence, perform timely gap filling, and control competitive broadleaf weeds before they rob nutrients.

Action Checklist

  • • Fill gaps within 7-10 days of sowing to ensure uniform plant canopy.
  • • Conduct first manual weeding or inter-cultivation with bullock/tractor hoe at 15-20 days.
  • • Provide light life irrigation if dry spell occurs right after sowing.
04

Observe & Nurture

Vegetative and flowering stages need balanced top-dressing and regular scouting for pests. Avoid preventative prophylactic spraying of hard chemicals; rely on IPM practices.

Action Checklist

  • • Split nitrogen top dressing in 2-3 installments rather than single heavy dumps.
  • • Install yellow/blue sticky traps and pheromone traps to scout pest thresholds.
  • • Spray 19:19:19 or micro-nutrient mixture during pre-flowering for vigorous fruit setting.
05

Harvest at Right Time

Premature harvesting leads to shriveled grains and lower oil content. Overdue harvesting causes pod shattering and bird damage. Track physiological maturity indicators carefully.

Action Checklist

  • • Stop irrigation 10-14 days before harvest to firm up soil and synchronize crop ripening.
  • • Harvest in clear sunshine; avoid harvesting wet plants to prevent fungal mold.
  • • Use sharp, clean threshing machinery adjusted to avoid seed coat cracking.
06

Protect the Value

Up to 15% of crop value is lost post-harvest due to improper drying and insect infestation in godowns. Dry thoroughly, grade by size/purity, and track e-NAM mandi auctions.

Action Checklist

  • • Dry grains on tarpaulins to safe moisture limits (Paddy: 13-14%, Pulses: 9-10%, Chillies: 10%).
  • • Store in clean, airtight gunny bags placed on wooden dunnage away from damp walls.
  • • Compare local yard prices with e-NAM digital quotes before closing transactions.
