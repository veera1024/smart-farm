Selecting Certified Varieties for Andhra Pradesh

Always purchase seed certified by the Andhra Pradesh State Seeds Development Corporation (APSSDC) or registered agricultural seed depots. Certified tags guarantee seed purity, germination percentages above 85%, and freedom from noxious weed seeds.

Seed treatments with Rhizobium (for pulses), Trichoderma viride, or imidacloprid protect young seedlings from damping-off and early sucking pests for up to 30 days.
