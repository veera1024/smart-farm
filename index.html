Proven Agronomy

Cultivation From Seed to Harvest

Follow our six practical stages to optimize yields, prevent pest outbreaks, and protect the market value of your harvest.

01

Know Your Soil

Test representative soil samples. Review pH, organic carbon, micro-nutrients, and drainage before investing in high-value seeds.

02

Prepare & Plant

Deep summer plowing destroys weed seeds and soil-borne pupae. Sow certified seeds treated with bio-fungicides at the right depth.

03

Help It Establish

Monitor germination within 7-10 days, fill gaps early, and maintain light moisture so young root networks penetrate vigorously.

04

Observe & Nurture

Practice Integrated Pest Management (IPM). Inspect undersides of leaves weekly and seek KVK advice before spraying insecticides.

05

Harvest at Right Time

Look for physiological maturity indicators like pod color and grain hardness. Harvest during dry weather to curb post-harvest fungal rot.

06

Protect the Value

Sun-dry produce to crop-specific safe moisture levels (10-12%). Clean, grade, and store in airtight bags to secure premium Mandi rates.
